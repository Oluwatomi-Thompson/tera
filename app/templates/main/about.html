About Tera

Tera exists to close one specific gap: most freely available investing education assumes you're in the US or UK, with foreign currencies, foreign brokers and foreign minimums. A young Rwandan researching "how to start investing" has to do a lot of extra translation work before any of it is actually usable.

Tera teaches the basics in RWF, treats mobile money as the payment method most Rwandans already use every day, and names real local institutions and thresholds instead of generic international ones.

What Tera is not

  • Not a broker. We never hold funds or execute trades.
  • Not personal financial advice. Lessons are educational, not a recommendation for your situation.
  • Not affiliated with the CMA, RSE or RNIT. We link to them as independent, publicly verifiable sources.

The funnel model

Once you've worked through the lessons and feel ready, Tera's job is done. We send you to the real, licensed institutions to open an account and invest. We'd rather be a good on-ramp than a mediocre bank.

A student project

Tera is built for an ALU software engineering course. It's a genuine attempt at a real problem, scoped so that one developer can ship and maintain it responsibly.
